Our Story

The Swipe That Almost Never Happened

01

Leipzig

Jyothsna set Bumble to Leipzig only. Tobias lived in Berlin, but came to Leipzig on a motorcycle tour. Otherwise, they may never have seen each other.

02

Berlin

After two weeks of phone calls, they met in Berlin. The plan was a motorcycle ride if they vibed. Instead, they spent the whole weekend together.

03

Motorcycle Adventures

The first motorcycle trip came the following summer, turning a shared love for the road into one of their favourite memories.

04

Budapest

On an island in the Danube, Tobias proposed with a diamond ring. When Jyothsna asked about their “diamonds are overrated” conversation, he said, “I think you are worth the diamonds.”
